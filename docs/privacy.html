LumaShield privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

LumaShield is a blue light filter and eye care app published by the LumaShield

team. This policy explains exactly what the app does with your information. The

short version: it stays on your phone.

What LumaShield stores

On your device, in the app private storage:

* filter settings (intensity, colour temperature, custom colour, dim),

* schedule settings (times, weekdays, optional approximate coordinates),

* eye care preferences (reminder intervals, quiet hours, sleep time),

* saved presets and per-app profiles,

* the times when protection was active, used to build your reports.

What LumaShield never collects

* Screen content, screenshots or recordings.

* Keystrokes, clipboard content or messages.

* Contacts, photos, files or calendars.

* Precise location history. Coordinates are read once, only if you enable

sunset scheduling, and are stored on the device.

* Advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising SDK.

* Account information. There is no account and no sign in.

Network access

The app declares no internet permission. It cannot upload data even in

principle. Reports, presets and history are local only.

Permissions explained

* **Display over other apps** - required to draw the warm overlay above other

apps. Without it the app cannot filter anything and says so.

* **Notifications** - required by Android for the foreground protection service,

and used for eye care reminders.

* **Battery optimisation exemption** - optional, keeps the service from being

killed in the background.

* **Exact alarms** - optional, lets schedules start on the minute.

* **Usage access** - optional, reads the package name of the foreground app so

per-app profiles can work. Screen content is not accessible with this

permission and is never read.

* **Approximate location** - optional, used once to calculate sunset and sunrise

offline.

* **Biometrics** - optional app lock. The check happens in the system biometric

prompt; the app only receives pass or fail.

Diagnostics

Anonymous diagnostics are **off by default**. If you turn them on, only the app

version, crash type and device model are sent, never screen content. You can turn

them off at any time in Advanced settings.

Children

LumaShield is a general wellbeing tool, not directed at children under 13, and

does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Your control

Clear protection history at any time in Advanced settings. Uninstalling the app

deletes every stored value. Because there is no server, there is no remote copy to

request or delete.

Medical disclaimer

LumaShield is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

If you have persistent eye pain, headaches or sleep problems, speak to a qualified

professional.
